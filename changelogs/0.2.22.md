## Changes

- Added `evaluateMssrRoutingComplianceOperationalAttention`, a pure portable projection over route state, trace state, lifecycle boundary, required/loaded skills, required/completed phases, trace replacement, and evidence completeness.
- Ordinary unrouted observations remain quiet `WATCH`; substantial tool work without routing, missing/ambiguous trace attribution, phase-boundary required-skill misses, and premature trace replacement become `REVIEW`; trace mismatch and invalid outcome closure conditions become `ERROR` where attribution/closure safety is at risk.
- Required-skill recommendations are exact and deterministic. Optional selected-but-not-loaded skills are not compliance failures and never become synthetic obligations.
- Routing-compliance fingerprints are semantic and order-independent; volatile host identifiers are excluded from policy identity.
- Recovery output is advisory only: the portable projection may recommend route/bootstrap, trace inspection/replan, required-skill loading, phase completion, or prior-outcome review, but it never executes or authorizes those actions.
- Bridge may preserve producer-facing compatibility codes while using this portable projection for severity, stable-state suppression, material-change notification, and explicit resolution.
- Added focused fixtures for clean routing, quiet ordinary unrouted evidence, substantial unrouted review, missing/ambiguous/mismatched traces, required-skill and required-phase misses, optional-skill negatives, outcome-without-route, premature trace replacement, incomplete evidence, deterministic ordering, stable-state suppression, material fingerprint changes, and resolution.
