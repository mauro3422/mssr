## Near-match diagnostics

- `planSkillRoute(...)` now returns bounded advisory `nearMatches` for structured-semantic candidates that remain blocked by explicit routing gates despite a strong partial match, or when the canonical skill name is explicitly mentioned.
- A near-match carries only bounded candidate score, matched dimensions and missing gate metadata (`dimension`, reason, accepted values and missing values). It is diagnostic evidence, not a routed skill.
- Near-matches never enter `loadOrder`, never satisfy a required skill, never bypass `requireNeedMatch` / `requireActionMatch` / `requireArtifactMatch` / `requireSignalMatch`, and never change host-gated optional-root semantics.
- The structured explicit-name rule remains unchanged: naming a skill raises ranking but does not override semantic gates. Lexical fallback retains its compatibility behavior and is not used as recovery for structured routing.
- Strong partial diagnostics are conservative: at least two semantic dimensions must match, diagnostic score must be at least 50, and no more than two blocking gates may be missing. Output is bounded to at most the smaller of eight or the route skill budget.
- `routingDiagnostics` makes the recovery policy explicit: validate the missing evidence against the actual task and perform at most one structured re-plan; never copy suggested values mechanically or bypass gates.

## Host guidance

- `mssr-agent-routing` and `docs/AGENT_PROTOCOL.md` now teach hosts to consume `nearMatches` as a bounded repair signal rather than silently losing a likely capability.
- Jev/System-One remains outside the base routing authority. No model call was added inside the router and the router remains deterministic after caller-supplied structured intent.
- The change generalizes beyond Jev: it applies to any routed skill with strict structured gates, while preserving the existing versioned routing metadata as activation authority.
