## Changes

- Advances the Architecture Impact host-adoption coordinator contract to schema v2. `planArchitectureHostAdoption(...)` may now expose sparse authority anchors, existing host-neutral symbol-analysis plans, derived-graph capability metadata, and exact invariant rules, while fixing `analyzersRequired=false` and preserving exact touched-ref scope.
- `evaluateArchitectureHostAdoption(...)` keeps the coarse C2f-C projection as the base evidence fact, then optionally reuses existing structural refinement, derived graph, invariant evaluation, reviewed-current, and bounded context-feedback contracts rather than reimplementing their semantics in a host.
- Compatible unchanged structural fingerprints may refine coarse `possible-impact` attention to WATCH without erasing the coarse projection; changed or unresolved structural landmarks remain REVIEW.
- Derived graph evidence remains `relationshipClass=derived`, candidate-only, `canonicalReviewEligible=false`, and cannot elevate aggregate architecture attention or widen declared refs.
- Repository-declared invariant evidence may raise aggregate attention to WATCH or REVIEW. A coarse exact reviewed-current receipt suppresses only the repeated coarse review and cannot suppress a newer structural or invariant REVIEW.
- Aggregate REVIEW reuses C2f-D exact `contextRef` + authority feedback at a natural replan. WATCH triggers no forced replan or context load. `semanticOwner=mssr`, `canonicalRewriteAllowed=false`, and host-owned observation remain invariant.
- Self-host structure metadata now fingerprints the coordinator's plan/evaluate symbols, and ADR/roadmap/selective Architecture Impact decision context document the completed B boundary. Native/Codex/OpenCode/Bridge runtime adoption remains gates C/D.
