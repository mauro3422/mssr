## Changes

- Centralizes the objective successful-outcome phase set in `trace-contract.ts`: `verification` and `persistence` are the independently observable direct closure gates. Discovery, safety and implementation may be represented by the route/host workflow, while maintenance keeps its separate close-revision obligation.
- Makes the C2b routing-compliance projection reuse that same lifecycle authority instead of independently interpreting every `requiredPhase`. This removes the contradictory path where an outcome with valid verification/persistence evidence could still be rejected for `safety`, `implementation`, `discovery`, or `maintenance`.
- Adds `evaluateMssrToolFrictionOperationalAttention(...)`, a pure host-neutral projection over bounded failure observations. Hosts provide a sanitized stable `toolName + signature`, timestamp and optional workflow/trace identity; portable MSSR groups identical friction and ranks it by recurrence, workflow breadth, recency, and severity.
- Repeated clusters project advisory `repeated-friction` attention owned by `skill-maintenance-loop`, while isolated failures remain lower-priority `watch` evidence. The evaluator never consumes raw prompts/arguments/stacks/secrets, never rewrites a tool or skill, and remains separate from routing-quality benchmark scores.
- Adds deterministic regression evidence modeled after the observed Bridge incident: 44 identical `work_once` schema failures across 8 workflows collapse into one `error` maintenance item with priority 100, while a single isolated failure remains `watch`.
