- Areas: project-knowledge, document-freshness, roadmaps, host-adapter contract, maintenance
- PROJECT_CONTEXT: updated
- PROJECT_MEMORY: updated
- PROJECT_STATE: updated

## Changes

- Add strict `.mssr/document-freshness.json` manifest/observation schemas and `evaluateDocumentFreshness(...)` as a pure host-neutral evaluator.
- Exact project-relative refs only: no absolute paths, traversal, fragments, globs, duplicate document identities, duplicate impact refs or self-dependencies.
- Evaluation is deliberately weaker than semantic contradiction detection: `impact-ref-newer` means the declared document is review-due; `semanticContradictionProven=false`, `canonicalRewriteAllowed=false`, and every result is advisory-only.
- `WATCH` represents unknown/missing bounded observation; `REVIEW` represents missing declared files or a declared impact ref proven newer than the reviewed document. Historical documents stay out of the manifest rather than being continuously checked.
- MSSR self-hosts the contract through `.mssr/document-freshness.json`, tracking the root roadmap and routing roadmap against their explicit current-state inputs.
- Reconcile stale roadmap text: Architecture Impact host adoption is marked complete because A–E are already complete, while the old `0.2.8` / Bridge `0.6.88` routing section is explicitly historical instead of claiming a restart is still pending.
- `AGENTS.md` and PROJECT_* authorities now record the evidence-only ownership boundary so freshness signals can request review but cannot mutate canonical project knowledge.
