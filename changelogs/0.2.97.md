## Jev probability-aware shortlist

- Preserve the optional per-option `Choice.probabilities` map through the TypeSafe Jev adapter. Validate exact option keys, approximate normalization, finite [0,1] probabilities, and that the selected Choice has a highest probability.
- For oversized heading catalogs, partition within the 254-candidate and 80,000 candidate-evidence-character per-call bounds. Retain the two leading candidates by a valid per-shard distribution, then make one global Choice. Providers without a valid distribution retain their selected local candidate. A planned total over 16 calls fails closed before provider use.
- Keep search, selection, exact fetch, relation review and reversible synthesis preview as explicit composable operations. Search remains caller-supplied and deterministic; exact fetch still revalidates revision/range/fingerprint. - Normalize text in grapheme-like base-plus-combining-mark clusters for retrieval snippets and Jev excerpt centering. Composed/decomposed accents match while exact source excerpts remain unchanged; Spanish `ñ` remains distinct from `n`.
- Document the different evidence scope seen by Noul in single-pass versus hierarchical mode. Both Choice confidence and Noul sufficiency remain uncalibrated for MSSR policy and are not pass thresholds.

## Controlled live evidence

Four runs use byte-identical frozen inputs: 21 MSSR docs, 200 headings and 26 English/Spanish query pairs. Two 80k direct runs scored 35/52 (18 EN, 17 ES) and 34/52 (17 EN, 17 ES); exact fetch passed 50/50 in both. The paired 64k hierarchical ablation scored 31/52, with 49/49 exact fetch, three abstentions, 156 provider calls and higher observed latency. A prior 96k synthetic hierarchy request exceeded TypeSafe's request limit; that failure remains recorded. The 80k direct results vary by one English exact-label case on repeat and do not establish a repeatable gain over the prior full-heading 34/52 score.

The labels are Luna-reviewed, not approved by the human document owner; alternate useful ranges and abstention quality are not labelled. The 261-heading live hierarchy smoke is synthetic and validates only the execution shape. Preserve inputs, raw responses, scorer outputs, threshold failures and per-run manifests/checksums under `experiments/jev-mssr-live/runs/`. See [the comparison and next evaluation gate](../experiments/jev-mssr-live/runs/MSSR-LIBRARIAN-JEV-COMPARISON-20261001.md).
