# Semantic consistency relations and retrieval decision

Typed semantic relations are directional, scope-bound and provenance-bearing. Only current `declared` `mirrors`/`summarizes` edges may authorize deterministic comparison between differently named subjects; `derived` relationships remain candidates. Required lifecycle relations such as `produces`/`consumes`/`adopts`/`implements` may prove an unresolved target/ref but never synthesize a missing target.

Candidate retrieval is a separate bounded stage: declared edges, same subjects and exact refs outrank TF-IDF; every lexical/derived result stays `CANDIDATE` with `truthAuthority=false`. `roadmap-contradiction` is produced only from PROVEN deterministic current roadmap evidence, while `unresolved-reference` comes only from explicit unresolved declared relations. Stable dedupe identity and resolution-to-silence are required to avoid repeated notice noise.

The optional model boundary is shadow-only. Host-supplied NLI/cross-encoder observations are normalized as `derived`/`candidate` evidence with `routingInfluence=false`, `truthAuthority=false`, `directNoticeAuthority=false`, and `canonicalRewriteAllowed=false`. A shadow contract is not empirical model validation or permission to promote model output.
