## Architecture impact review decision

Situation Model C2f uses repository-declared architecture relationships plus non-widening structural evidence. `.mssr/architecture-impact.json` owns exact declared authority/impact refs; hosts only observe current evidence and portable MSSR owns comparison/evaluation semantics. A linked or structurally changed source is never proof that architecture changed, and only explicit maintenance may update ADR/context/link authorities.

Architecture Impact host adoption is closed across the portable coordinator and the deliberately separate host gates. `planArchitectureHostAdoption` may expose already-declared Markdown anchors, host-neutral symbol-analysis plans and exact invariant rules, but analyzers remain optional host observation capabilities (`analyzersRequired=false`). `evaluateArchitectureHostAdoption` preserves coarse `possible-impact` as evidence while compatible unchanged structural fingerprints may lower attention to WATCH; changed/unresolved landmarks remain REVIEW; derived graph edges remain non-authoritative candidates and never raise canonical attention; declared invariant evidence may raise WATCH/REVIEW. A coarse reviewed-current receipt suppresses only that exact coarse repeated review and cannot silence newer structural or invariant REVIEW. Aggregate REVIEW reuses the declared `contextRef` plus authority/ADR at a natural replan under normal budgets. The coordinator remains `semanticOwner=mssr`, performs no host I/O, and fixes `canonicalRewriteAllowed=false`. Bridge/ChatGPT Web adoption is proven live; Codex, native and OpenCode expose the same I/O-free plan/evaluate boundary under the shared portable semantics. Host package/runtime adoption is still proved separately from source completion rather than inferred from package parity. Detailed release-by-release history lives in `architecture-impact-review-history.md` and should be loaded only for debugging/history recovery.
