## Architecture impact review decision

Situation Model C2f uses repository-declared architecture relationships plus non-widening structural evidence. `.mssr/architecture-impact.json` owns exact declared authority/impact refs; hosts only observe current evidence and portable MSSR owns comparison/evaluation semantics. A linked or structurally changed source is never proof that architecture changed, and only explicit maintenance may update ADR/context/link authorities.

Current closure through 0.2.43: derived graph evidence remains non-authoritative; executable invariants are explicitly declared and never auto-rewrite architecture; active REVIEW may request only the declared `contextRef` plus authority/ADR at a natural replan under normal budgets; reviewed-current receipts are host-local metadata under ignored `.mssr/runtime/`, require explicit review over complete `possible-impact` evidence, suppress only an exact declaration/baseline/authority/source-set/projection/integrity match, and fail open to REVIEW when missing, corrupt, incomplete or changed. Bridge-only graph/AST tools may observe but never own portable semantics. The portable C2f source gate is complete, but automatic pre-edit/replan consumption by native/Codex/OpenCode/Bridge is a separate host-adoption gate; each host must prove packaged-version/runtime adoption and parity instead of treating Bridge-local metadata as portable truth. Detailed release-by-release history lives in `architecture-impact-review-history.md` and should be loaded only for debugging/history recovery.
