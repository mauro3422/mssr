# Project Context Plane architecture

MSSR owns project-context meaning, selection, budgets, health and maintenance. `.mssr/` is the only active project-control home: compact PROJECT_* authorities plus selective `.mssr/knowledge/<topic>/` modules. Bridge/hosts own authorized I/O and transport, never context meaning.

`kind` defines authority (`context`, `memory`, `state`, scoped `directive`); `topic`/`area` classify it. Optional memory is reference-backed so `PROJECT_MEMORY.md` stays compact. `.mssr/runtime/` is reconstructable state, never durable truth.

Initialization establishes the contract. Health reports invalid/legacy state, missing sources, unindexed knowledge and pressure. WATCH starts at 75% of `maxChars`, REVIEW at 90%; budgets are not raised to hide growth. Document discovery remains advisory and does not establish ownership.

## Maintenance and write preflight

Write preflight measures selectable bytes and rejects overflow. Segmented/ref-backed parents budget worst selectable payload separately from physical growth. Growth into REVIEW is blocked; shrinking pressured sources remains valid.

Structural maintenance is automatic only for provable exact-byte moves with safe boundaries. A pressured optional module may keep a compact baseline plus selective refs in `.mssr/project-context-refs.json`; parent identity/selectors remain authoritative, selection loads baseline plus at most one uniquely best ref, and internal segments cannot mix with external refs.

Jev/System-One is advisory. Ref-split keeps parent authority fixed and evaluates exact sections; auto-move requires independent evidence that content is safely selective while current/protected or ambiguous material stays baseline/review. Apply remains confirmed, hash-bound and exact-byte; semantic rewrites and invented boundaries are never automatic.

Semantic review has no apply authority. Deterministic retrieval proposes bounded evidence; Shef/Jev decisions enter the Semantic Experience Layer as compact shadows for relation, context-selection, document-role, placement, drift, maintenance or abstention. Only independent confirmed/corrected evidence can train fallback rules. From 0.2.87, the existing privacy-safe `learning-digest-v1` is also the automatic host-neutral experience bus: context selections, bounded drift findings, maintenance disposition and abstention are projected with trace/workflow identity without loading raw traces or source text. Retries dedupe, contradictions stay review-only, and fallback abstains without a verified rule. Fallback preference is gated by leave-one-project-out evidence (minimum verified holdout/project breadth/precision/coverage and zero tolerated wrong decisions); passing that gate still grants no authority, routing, canonical rewrite or auto-apply.

## Cross-cutting mutation contracts

A module may declare `requiredWhen: { mutation: true, artifacts?: [...] }`. Matching structured mutation intent makes it required before ranking; read-only work does not. Applicability is repository-declared and budgeted, never inferred from prose and never permission to mutate.
