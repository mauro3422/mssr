Situation Model C2f uses coarse declared relations plus sparse structural refinement. C2f-C.5-D (0.2.41) separates non-authoritative derived graph candidates from repository-declared executable invariants: import/call/dependency edges may suggest nearby refs only when anchored from an already declared architecture ref, remain `relationshipClass=derived` with `canonicalReviewEligible=false`, and never widen canonical scope; `.mssr/architecture-invariants.json` is an explicit declared authority for machine-checkable `require-edge`/`forbid-edge` rules, where complete evidence can prove presence/absence, partial evidence may prove an observed edge but never absence, and violations produce REVIEW evidence with `canonicalRewriteAllowed=false`. C2f-D (0.2.42) owns bounded architecture-context feedback by reusing C2e-E exact resolution only at natural MSSR replans: active REVIEW may recommend at most the declared `contextRef` plus architecture authority/ADR, every request remains advisory and `required=false`, aligned architecture emits nothing, and the feedback explicitly forbids semantic retrieval reruns, silent auto-loads, budget overrides, and per-tool-response injection. C2f-E (0.2.43) owns the portable reviewed-current receipt contract while each host owns persistence under ignored `.mssr/runtime/`: receipt creation requires an explicit `reviewed-current` decision over complete active `possible-impact` evidence; exact declaration/baseline/authority/source-set/projection identity plus receipt-integrity match is required to suppress only the same repeated review. Missing/corrupt receipts, incomplete evidence, or any identity change fail open to REVIEW; local receipt loss may cause safe re-review and no receipt may edit canonical architecture. Bridge-only graph/AST tools may implement host observation but never portable authority. A linked or structurally changed source is never proof that architecture changed; only explicit maintenance may update an ADR/context/link manifest.
