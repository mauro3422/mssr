## First-party core skill direction

MSSR-owned operational skills have one canonical bundled source for the five names in `config/first-party-skills.json`. Native, Codex-local, and OpenCode-local source providers discover that package with reserved-name precedence; a custom catalog may not reclaim one of those names as an editable owner. The package boundary does not imply loading every first-party skill on every task; required/on-demand/closing activation remains explicit. In 0.2.54 each root becomes a compact control plane and selects direct parent-owned references through its manifest; routing metadata/fixtures remain unchanged. The v1 distribution gate proves package dry-run, routing/context selection, safe opt-in junction migration and audit without publishing externally.
